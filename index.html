The Kottu Kade Philosophy

WHY WE ONLY SERVE 3 KOTTUS

At KOTTU KADE, we refused to create a 50-page menu. Instead, we spent years perfecting the exact heat on the iron grid, the ideal spice roast ratio, and the ultimate mozzarella blend that creates the iconic stringy cheese pull.

High-Heat Chopped Fresh

Every plate is freshly chopped to order using twin blades on custom-forged steel plates.

100% Real Dairy Cheese

No artificial cheese sauces. Only premium block mozzarella & golden cheddar baked directly into the hot roti grid.
